About The Program

Empowering Student Developers with Google Cloud

Google Cloud Study Jams 2026 is an inclusive, student-first learning initiative organized by Google Developer Group at B. P. Poddar Institute of Management & Technology (BPPIMT). Designed to bridge academic study with real-world cloud engineering, the program offers a structured, supportive environment for learners at every stage.

Program Roadmap

Your Learning Journey

A progressive, four-stage pathway designed to guide students from initial concepts to confident cloud experimentation.

01
Step 1

Get Started

Understand cloud computing fundamentals, global infrastructure, IAM access controls, and how to navigate the Google Cloud Console.

02
Step 2

Learn by Doing

Explore guided learning resources, deploy lightweight HTTP Cloud Functions, and manage unstructured static assets on Cloud Storage.

03
Step 3

Build & Experiment

Apply concepts through hands-on activities: wire event-driven triggers, manage persistent Firestore documents, and test schema validation.

04
Step 4

Learn Together

Collaborate in peer study circles, participate in mentor Q&A sessions, review code with classmates, and share your project outcomes.

Community & Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding participation, prerequisites, and program flow.

Who can participate?

Google Cloud Study Jams 2026 is open to all students across all engineering and management departments at B. P. Poddar Institute of Management & Technology (BPPIMT), regardless of academic year.

Do I need prior Google Cloud experience?

No prior cloud or Google Cloud experience is necessary. The program is specifically structured to introduce cloud fundamentals from the beginning before progressing to hands-on exercises.

How does registration work?

Fill out the student registration form on this page. Your registration is validated and recorded securely by our serverless Google Cloud backend. Study schedule announcements will be shared with registered students via email.

How can I volunteer as a mentor?

If you have experience with Google Cloud, software development, or cloud architecture, you can submit the "Become a Mentor" form above. The Google Developer Group team will review applications and coordinate mentor orientation.

When does the program begin?

The Study Jams program is slated to start in November 2026 and continue through the designated study schedule. Confirmed dates and meeting times will be communicated to registered participants.
